2D Ising model

The 2D Ising model is the simplest model of a magnet. It is a grid of tiny magnets, called spins, and each spin points up, shown in teal, or down, shown in white. Neighboring spins prefer to point the same way, while heat flips them at random. At low temperature the spins line up into large patches, and above about 2.27 the patches break up into noise. Magnetization is the average spin, which runs from −1 when all spins point down to +1 when all point up.

The mark Tc at about 2.269 is the critical temperature, where the order breaks down on a very large grid.
