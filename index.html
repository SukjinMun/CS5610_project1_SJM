Suk Jin Mun

M.S. student in Data Science at Northeastern University, working on machine learning for materials.

I build machine learning methods that speed up materials discovery and characterization. I earned a B.S. in Physics at HKUST and later analyzed X-ray and neutron diffraction data at the Institute for Basic Science in Korea.

Today I am a research assistant in the D2R2 Lab with Prof. Peter Schindler. I code in Python, MATLAB, R, C++, and Bash, and run DFT with Quantum ESPRESSO.

Highlights

Current research

In the D2R2 Lab I use equivariant graph neural networks to predict work functions of 2D materials. I also screen switchable sliding ferroelectrics with DFT and machine learning.

My M.S. thesis builds a generative model for adsorbate structures, co-advised by Prof. Robin Walters.

Research experience

RAPID

RAPID is a CNN-based program I developed that automates Rietveld refinement of powder XRD data. It appeared in the Journal of Applied Crystallography in 2026.

The Bragg peak explorer on my research page shows where the diffraction peaks of a cubic crystal fall.
