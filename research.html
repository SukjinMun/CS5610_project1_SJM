Research

I use data science and machine learning to study materials and to design new ones.

Research interests

  • Machine learning for finding and designing new materials
  • Automating the analysis of materials data

Experience

  1. D2R2 Lab, Northeastern University

    Research Assistant with Prof. Peter Schindler, Sep 2025 to present

    • I build machine learning models that predict the properties of materials.
    • My M.S. thesis builds a machine learning model that proposes material structures, and Prof. Robin Walters of the Geometric Learning Lab co-advises it.

  2. Center for Van der Waals Quantum Solids, Institute for Basic Science, Pohang

    Research Assistant in Dr. Sungkyun Choi's group, June 2024 to July 2025

    • I built RAPID, a machine learning tool that automates part of the analysis of materials data. I published it as first author in 2026.
    • I wrote a MATLAB pipeline that ranks computer simulations by how well they match measured data.

  3. Center for Integrated Nanostructure Physics, Institute for Basic Science, Suwon

    Research Assistant in Dr. Sungkyun Choi's group, Feb 2023 to May 2023

    • I wrote Python tools for data and image analysis.
    • My analysis contributed to a 2025 paper in Physical Review Research.

  4. Thin Film Technology Group, Max Planck Institute for Solid State Research, Stuttgart

    Research Assistant with Dr. Gennady Logvenov and Dr. Gideok Kim, June to Aug 2019 and Jan to Feb 2020

    • I measured the electrical and magnetic behavior of material samples and analyzed the results.

  5. Yonsei University

    Research Intern in Prof. Aeree Chung's group, Sep 2018 to Jan 2019

    • I analyzed telescope data on gas-rich galaxies and their surroundings.

  6. Seoul National University

    Research Intern in Prof. Sascha Trippe's group, Mar to May 2018

    • I processed data from a network of radio telescopes.
